    Planes, trains and the open road

    Getting here

    Cassopolis sits in the middle of the St. Joseph River valley, an easy drive from Chicago, Detroit, Indianapolis and Grand Rapids, with an airport at South Bend and Amtrak stations at Dowagiac and Niles, two of the valley’s handsomest old depots.

    Times are estimates without traffic, worked out from road routes between town centers. Allow extra time around Chicago, where the clock is also an hour behind.

    By air

    • South Bend International Airport (SBN), about 27 miles, 43 min by car to Cassopolis. The closest airport; the South Shore Line train to Chicago leaves from the airport station. Airport site
    • Chicago O'Hare International Airport (ORD), about 135 miles, 3 h 13 min by car to Cassopolis. Chicago is on Central time, one hour behind Cassopolis. Traffic can add an hour or more. Airport site
    • Chicago Midway International Airport (MDW), about 115 miles, 2 h 26 min by car to Cassopolis. Central time. Airport site
    • Kalamazoo/Battle Creek International Airport (AZO), about 45 miles, 1 h 01 min by car to Cassopolis. Airport site
    • Gerald R. Ford International Airport (Grand Rapids) (GRR), about 94 miles, 1 h 55 min by car to Cassopolis. Airport site

    By train

    • Niles Amtrak station (NLS): Wolverine (Chicago–Detroit–Pontiac, three daily round trips) and Blue Water (Chicago–Port Huron, one daily round trip). Station building with waiting room, parking, accessible platform. The Michigan Central built the depot in 1892 (Spier and Rohns, architects); its tower and gardens are a local landmark. History source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niles_station_(Michigan) Station page
    • Dowagiac Amtrak station (DOA): Wolverine and Blue Water. The nearest station to Cassopolis. Depot restored in the mid-1990s after its listing on the National Register of Historic Places (Amtrak). Station page
    • Kalamazoo Transportation Center (KAL): Wolverine and Blue Water; also Greyhound and Indian Trails buses. Station page
    • South Bend Amtrak station (SOB): Lake Shore Limited (Chicago–New York/Boston) and the Floridian (Chicago–Miami; Amtrak calls this merged train temporary). Not on the Michigan Wolverine/Blue Water line. Station page
    • South Shore Line (commuter rail to Chicago): Electric commuter trains between South Bend Airport and Millennium Station in downtown Chicago, with stops including Hudson Lake and Michigan City. A future relocation of the South Bend station is planned but no date is set; check service notices (in October 2026 some Chicago stops were bus-substituted). Station page

    By bus

    • Greyhound – South Bend (South Street Station), 100 W. South Street, Suite 120, South Bend, IN 46601. Curbside stop outside the Transpo South Street Station.
    • Greyhound/FlixBus – Benton Harbor stop, 2412 M-139, Benton Harbor, MI 49022. Greyhound notes the ticket carries the most current address.
    • Kalamazoo Transportation Center (Greyhound, Indian Trails), 459 North Burdick Street, Kalamazoo, MI 49007. Indian Trails also partners with Megabus on Michigan routes.

    Clocks

    All of southwest Michigan (Berrien, Cass, Van Buren, St. Joseph, Kalamazoo) and Indiana's St. Joseph and Elkhart counties keep Eastern time. LaPorte County, Indiana (Michigan City and La Porte), keeps Central time, as does Chicago — so the clock jumps an hour when you cross into LaPorte County or ride the South Shore Line west of it.
