    Then and now

    South Bend, 1863 and today

    The 1863 map of St. Joseph County, Indiana, has a plan of South Bend in its corner, with the village of Lowell across the river. Here it is laid over the streets of today. Fade between the two, or drag a line across and see one beside the other.

    Things to look for

    • Michigan, Main and Lafayette Streets, and the Court House square on Main. Fade to today and the square is still there.
    • Water Street and Market Street. Both are still on the ground, under other names. Fade to today and find what they are called now.
    • Lowell, across the river: a separate town in 1863, part of South Bend today.
    • The great bend of the St. Joseph River, and the bridges across it.
    • The Agricultural Fair Ground, and the Cemetery beside the La Porte road at the west edge of town.
    • The additions named for the men who laid them out, such as Hendricks & Grant’s. Each name is a lead to a plat in the county’s records.

    Two streets that changed their names

    Water Street and Market Street are both still on the ground in South Bend, under the names they carry today. Fade the plan away and find them.

      How this was made

      The plan is shown live from the Library of Congress’s image service, at full detail. Eighteen street corners that appear on both maps (Marion, Madison, Washington, Jefferson and Wayne, where they meet William, Lafayette, Main and Michigan) were matched by measurement, and the plan was fitted to them, turning it a fraction of a degree. On average the old corners land within about nine yards of the new ones. The worst is near William Street, which jogs on today’s ground and runs straight on the 1863 plan.

      The pale band across the plan is a fold in the paper of the original. Nothing has been painted out.

      1863 plan: Map of St. Joseph Co., Indiana, compiled and published by M. W. Stokes (South Bend, 1863), inset “South Bend” and “Lowell.” Library of Congress, Geography and Map Division, g4093s.la000168 (item 2013593163).
