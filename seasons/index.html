    Seasons & festivals

    When to come, and why to come back

    Here are 59 fairs, festivals, harvests, living-history days and holiday walks across the St. Joseph River valley, season by season. Most have run for decades; a few for more than a century. The season we are in comes first.

        Spring 9 events

        Blossoms, sap buckets and the first open days

        • March · Kalamazoo, MI

          Kalamazoo Living History Show

          Third weekend of March (approx.)

          A large juried show for reenactors and history buffs, where makers sell pre-1890 clothing, gear and crafts at the county Expo Center; open to the public, with a different reenactment group hosting each year.

          Founded 1976.

          A table nearby: Bell's Eccentric Café (in town) · Louie's Trophy House (in town)

          Their website › · ·

        • March · Kalamazoo, MI

          Kalamazoo Nature Center Maple Sugar Festival

          Early March, one Sunday, 9 am–4 pm

          A day in the sugarbush at the Nature Center, with a pancake breakfast served with Michigan maple syrup and horse-drawn wagon rides around the grounds.

          2027 is billed as the 61st annual festival; it began as 'a kettle on a tripod.'

          A table nearby: Bell's Eccentric Café (in town) · Louie's Trophy House (in town)

          Their website › · ·

        • March · New Carlisle, IN

          Sugar Camp Days at Bendix Woods

          Third weekend of March

          St. Joseph County Parks opens its sugar bush and sugar house for tours of sap gathering and boiling, with a Lions Club pancake breakfast, historical crafters, wagon rides and maple treats.

          Bendix Woods was once part of the Studebaker proving-ground complex; its clubhouse and the CCC-planted 'Studebaker' pine-tree sign (1930s) are on the National Register.

          Their website › · ·

        • April · Bristol, IN

          Bonneyville Mill season

          Mid-April to mid-November, Tuesday–Sunday

          Elkhart County Parks' grist mill, billed as Indiana's oldest operating grist mill, grinds grain and sells its flour through the warm months; the park adds schoolhouse open houses, a Feature Grain Day and autumn leaf walks.

          A table nearby: Das Dutchman Essenhaus (6 mi)

          Their website › ·

        • April · Wakarusa, IN

          Wakarusa Maple Syrup Festival

          Last full weekend of April, Friday–Sunday

          A three-day small-town festival with a Saturday parade, carnival rides, dozens of vendor booths and plenty of maple treats.

          A table nearby: Coppes Commons (7 mi)

          Their website › · ·

        • May · St. Joseph & Benton Harbor, MI

          Blossomtime Festival

          Late April to early May, ending with the Saturday Grand Floral Parade

          Southwest Michigan's spring festival honoring the fruit-growing country, shared by some two dozen communities, with the Blessing of the Blossoms and a Grand Floral Parade that crosses the river from St. Joseph into Benton Harbor.

          Established 1906 and described as Michigan's oldest multi-community festival; the Blessing of the Blossoms began in 1906 with Rev. W. J. Cady.

          A table nearby: The Livery (1 mi) · Bit of Swiss Pastry Shoppe (5 mi)

          Their website › · ·

        • May · Dowagiac, MIcheck the dates

          Dogwood Fine Arts Festival

          About ten days in mid-May

          Dowagiac's spring arts festival brings readings by visiting authors, concerts, art shows and a downtown art walk, and has commissioned public sculpture for the city.

          Began in 1995, pairing visiting sculptors with local donors who commissioned public art (City of Dowagiac).

          A table nearby: Wounded Minnow Saloon (in town) · Wood Fire Trattoria (in town)

          Their website › ·

        • May · Hesston (near La Porte), IN

          Hesston Steam Museum operating season

          Weekends, Memorial Day through Labor Day

          The La Porte County Historical Steam Society runs its steam and diesel railroads and antique machinery on summer weekends, opening with National Train Day and Memorial Day weekend.

          First show and reunion held in 1957; the steam society was chartered as a nonprofit in 1968.

          A table nearby: Redamak's (4 mi) · Oink's Dutch Treat (4 mi)

          Their website › ·

        • May · Shipshewana, IN

          Shipshewana Flea Market season

          Early May to late September, Tuesdays & Wednesdays

          The Trading Place's big open-air flea market runs two days a week all summer, alongside the weekly antique and livestock auctions in the auction barn.

          A table nearby: Blue Gate Restaurant (in town)

          Their website › · ·

          Lakeside Live

          Thursday evenings, June to August, 6–9 pm

          A different band every Thursday night on the beach at Stone Lake, all summer long. Bring a blanket and a friend.

          Organized by Cassopolis Main Street, the village’s downtown organization.

        • June · Elkhart County towns (Goshen, Nappanee, Middlebury, Elkhart and others), IN

          Quilt Gardens along the Heritage Trail

          Late May through early fall

          Seventeen giant flower gardens planted in traditional quilt patterns across Elkhart County, free to tour along the Heritage Trail through the summer.

          2026 is the program's 19th year.

          Their website ›

        • June · Three Oaks, MI

          Three Oaks Flag Day Celebration

          A weekend in early-to-mid June

          Billed as the world's largest Flag Day parade, Three Oaks turns out for a weekend of patriotic celebration honoring veterans and the flag.

          First parade June 14, 1953, organized by Herb Flick under American Legion Post 204; it grew to a three-day celebration in the late 1970s.

          A table nearby: Journeyman Distillery & Staymaker (in town) · Redamak's (7 mi)

          Their website › ·

        • June · Three Rivers, MI

          Three Rivers Water Festival

          Third weekend of June, Thursday–Saturday

          Three days downtown and at Scidmore Park: an opening parade, arts and crafts, a soap box derby, car show, duck race and fireworks over the rivers.

          The 2019 festival was the 63rd annual (City of Three Rivers).

          Their website › · ·

        • June · Vicksburg, MI

          Vicksburg Old Car Festival

          Second weekend of June (approx.), Friday–Saturday

          More than a thousand old cars, from Model Ts to hot rods, fill the village on Saturday, while the Vicksburg Historic Village opens its buildings and an antique tractor and gas-engine show runs on the grounds.

          No website: ask locally · ·

        • July · South Haven area, MI

          Blueberry u-pick season

          July through August (some varieties into mid-September)

          South Haven sits at the heart of Michigan's blueberry country, and through July and August farms and u-pick fields around town open for picking.

          The area claims the world's largest concentration of blueberry growers, nearly 300 farmers within 50 miles.

          A table nearby: Clementine's (in town)

          Their website › ·

          Elkhart County 4-H Fair

          Their website › · ·

          Mid-July, one week

          Established 1845; Pioneerland added in 1995.

        • August · Niles, MI

          Fort St. Joseph Archaeology Open House

          First weekend of August

          Each August the archaeologists excavating the site of the 18th-century French colonial fort on the St. Joseph River open their dig to the public, with artifact displays, 'ask an archaeologist' tables, guided tours and a living history village of costumed reenactors.

          Western Michigan University's Fort St. Joseph Archaeological Project has more than 25 years of research at the site.

          A table nearby: Thistledown & Row (5 mi) · Old Tavern Inn (6 mi)

          Their website › · ·

        Harvest, cider, colour and living history

          A one-day bicycle tour from Three Oaks through orchard and wine country, with routes from 15 to 100 miles and cider and fruit along the way.

        • September · Coloma, Berrien Springs, Galien, Three Oaks and other towns, MI

          Apple-picking season in the Fruit Belt

          Mid-August through October

          Berrien County's orchards open for u-pick apples from mid-August into October, many with farm markets, cider and wagon rides.

          Jollay Orchards in Coloma dates its farm to 1857.

          Their website ›

          Hesston Labor Day Weekend Steam & Power Show

          Labor Day weekend, four days

          The steam museum's biggest weekend: steam engines, antique machinery and trains in action, with a steam-powered threshing machine working the harvest.

          The first show and reunion was held in 1957.

          A table nearby: Redamak's (4 mi) · Oink's Dutch Treat (4 mi)

        • September · Vicksburg, MI

          Vicksburg Harvest Festival

          Last Sunday of September, 11 am–4 pm

          The Vicksburg Historical Society opens its Historic Village, general store, print shop and schoolhouse for a free day of crafts and fiber demonstrations, antique tractors, wagon rides and pumpkin decorating, celebrating the area's farm heritage.

          Candlelight Christmas Parade

          First Friday of December (approx.), evening

          Dowagiac's evening holiday parade brings well over a hundred lighted units down Front Street, with a tree lighting at Beckwith Park, as part of the town's 'Celebrate the Holidays' season.

          December, holiday self-guided tours

          The History Museum decorates the Oliver family's 38-room Copshaholm mansion with Christmas trees on all three floors and themed table settings, and opens it for self-guided holiday tours.

        • January · St. Joseph, MI

          St. Joseph Ice Fest

          A weekend in late January / early February

          Downtown St. Joseph fills with ice carvers working through the weekend, a lit 'Fire & Ice' tower, frozen games for families and a scavenger hunt along State and Pleasant streets.

        • January · Cassopolis, MI

          Stone Lake Ice Fishing Bowl

          A Saturday in January, when the ice is at least six inches thick; lines in at 6 am, fish measured by 11 am

          A hook-and-line contest for the longest bluegill, crappie and perch through the ice of Stone Lake, with check-in at the beach Snack Shack near 203 S. Disbrow and awards afterward on Broadway. Entry is $30 cash; the 2026 bowl was postponed for thin ice, so watch for the new date.

          Organized by Cassopolis Main Street, the village’s downtown organization.

          A table nearby: Holden Green Tavern (in town) · Wounded Minnow Saloon (7 mi)

        Before you go

        • Check the dates. Most events keep the same weekend each year, but the exact days are set by the organisers. Their own website has the latest.
        • Make a weekend of it. Pair an event with the museums nearby and somewhere to eat and stay.
        • Know of one we missed? Tell us, with its website.
