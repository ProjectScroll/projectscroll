    Case 7 · The second hall · A lake with no stream in and no stream out

    What powered the flour mill on a lake with no stream, and where did it stand?

    Open Nobody has cracked it yet.

    Opened 17 Oct 2026.

    Start here

    This case belongs to the second hall. Its sources are posted on the hall’s own page, and that page is behind the first hall’s last door. Find the door, and the trail starts there.

    How to answer

    1. Search the record. The first sources are on the second hall’s page, behind the first hall’s last door; then follow where they lead: old county histories, maps, certificates and newspapers, many of them free online.
    2. Post your answer in the comments on Project Scroll’s Facebook page, and say exactly where you found it: the book or record, the page, and a link if there is one.
    3. Get named. The first solver with the page to prove it is named in the next post and on this board.

    What counts as solved

    A case is solved when an answer is posted with the record it came from: the book or document, the page, and a link if there is one. The desk checks the page. If it says what you say it says, your name goes on the board, the case is marked Solved with your source beside it, and the next post names you. An answer without the page stays a guess, however right it is.

    A good answer looks like this: “Case 7: [your answer]. Found in [the book or record], p. [page], [link].” Use Copy as a Facebook answer in your notebook below, and it is laid out for you.

    Everything is solved on paper. Never dig, never go near a grave, and never set foot on anyone’s land.
