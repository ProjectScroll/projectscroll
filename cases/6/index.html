    Open Nobody has cracked it yet.

    Opened 17 Oct 2026.

    How to answer

    1. Search the record. Old county histories, maps, certificates and newspapers. Many are free online.
    2. Post your answer in the comments on Project Scroll’s Facebook page, and say exactly where you found it.
    3. Get named. The first solver with the page to prove it is named in the next post and on this board.

    Everything is solved on paper. Never dig, never go near a grave, and never set foot on anyone’s land.
