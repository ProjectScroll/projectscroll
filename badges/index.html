    Your tin badges

    In the winter of 1846–47, two hundred badges of the “Ancient and Honorable Order” were worn at a ball in Cassopolis. Not one is known to survive. Here you can earn your own: twenty of them, one for each thing you do as you explore the site, and one for cracking a case, with five ranks on the way from Candidate to Keeper of the Hall.

        Say the word

        Somewhere on every page of this site, a line is written in code. Turn it with the cipher wheel in the hunter’s toolkit, and it will tell you what to do.

        The second line

        You broke the first. Here is a harder one, and the cipher wheel alone will not open it. Look at the wheel’s other setting.

        GSV HVXLMW DLIW RH XSZIGVI. ML DSVVO DROO GFIM RG. SLOW RG GL Z NRIILI.
