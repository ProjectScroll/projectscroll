    Your tin badges

    In the winter of 1846–47, two hundred badges of the “Ancient and Honorable Order” were worn at a ball in Cassopolis. Not one is known to survive. Here, you can earn your own, one for each thing you do as you explore the site.

      Say the word

      Somewhere on every page of this site, a line is written in code. Turn it with the cipher wheel in the hunter’s toolkit, and it will tell you what to do.

      The second line

      You broke the first. Here is a harder one, and the cipher wheel alone will not open it. Look at the wheel’s other setting.

      GSV HVXLMW DLIW RH XIVWL. ML DSVVO DROO GFIM RG. SLOW RG GL Z NRIILI.
