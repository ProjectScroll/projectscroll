    Your passport

    Every place you have stood

    Press “I’m here” at a museum, a stop on a walk, a table or a festival, and its stamp lands here. Ten, twenty-five and fifty stamps each earn a badge, and the certificate is yours to print.

    0 stamps

    102550

    No stamps yet. Start with the museums, a walk, a table or this season’s festivals: each has an “I’m here” button that works when you are standing there.

      This season’s trail: the harvest

      Between September and November, stamp all three: the mill that grinds the grain, the orchard that has farmed the same ground since 1857, and the town where the stoves were made. All three in one fall earns the harvest badge.

      1. Bonneyville Mill, Bristol, Indiana
      2. Jollay Orchards, Coloma, Michigan
      3. Dowagiac Area History Museum, Dowagiac, Michigan

      A new trail opens each season. Check the mill’s and the museum’s hours before you go.

      A paper passport

      Prefer paper? Print the passport booklet: a box for every museum, walk stop, table and festival the site counts, to fill with stamps, signatures or dates as you go. Print it double-sided, fold it, and take it with you.
