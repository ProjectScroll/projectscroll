    Michigan

    • historic inn · Lakeside

      Lakeside Inn

      John and Nancy Aylsworth bought the site in 1901 and built the inn, probably in the early 1910s. After decades as a resort and an arts center, it reopened as a hotel in 1995 and was added to the National Register in 2009.

      A rambling century-old lakeside inn on the bluff.

      15251 Lakeshore Rd., Lakeside, MI 49116

      Nearby: Region of Three Oaks Museum, 4.4 mi away

      Their website › · Directions · ·

    • B&B on a historic estate · Nilescheck before you go

      The Morris Estate

      The riverside estate first belonged to E. M. Morris, a businessman known for planting trees and for his gifts to the community. Braden and Amber Janowski bought it in 2010 and opened it as a B&B in 2018.

      A riverside estate with guest rooms, weddings and events.

      1997 Morris Dr., Niles, MI 49120

      Nearby: Niles District Library – Local History & Genealogy, 2.2 mi away

      Their website › · Directions · ·

    • historic inn · Union Pier

      Gordon Beach Inn

      Chicago physician Louis Gordon built the inn in 1924 at a time when nearby lake towns turned away Jewish visitors. It is a State Historic Site and one of the last resort hotels of its era along this shore.

      A twenty-room 1920s resort inn in the woods near the beach, with a restaurant on site.

      16220 Lakeshore Rd., Union Pier, MI 49129

      Nearby: Harbor Country Museum of History + Rail (formerly New Buffalo Railroad Museum), 3.3 mi away

      Their website › · Directions · ·

    • inn · Union Pier

      The Inn at Union Pier

      In the 1920s the property was the Karonsky Hotel, a kosher resort for Chicago vacationers.

      A Harbor Country inn with antique Swedish tile stoves (kakelugns) in many rooms.

      9708 Berrien St., Union Pier, MI 49129

      Their website › · Directions

    • historic inn and restaurant · Kalamazoo

      Henderson Castle

      Frank Henderson, president of the Henderson-Ames Company, had the castle finished in 1895 to a design by C. A. Gombert of Milwaukee, at a cost of $72,000. Chef François Moyet bought it in 2011 and runs it as an inn.

      A hilltop stone mansion with 14 guest rooms, a chophouse restaurant, afternoon tea, tours and a vineyard.

      100 Monroe St., Kalamazoo, MI 49006

      Nearby: Mountain Home Cemetery, next door

      Their website › · Directions · ·

    • B&B · Kalamazoo

      Stuart Avenue Inn

      The inn is an 1886 Queen Anne house in Kalamazoo's Stuart Neighborhood Historic District.

      A Victorian bed-and-breakfast on a street of grand old houses.

      229 Stuart Ave., Kalamazoo, MI 49007

      Nearby: Kalamazoo College Archives & A.M. Todd Rare Book Room, next door

      Their website › · Directions · ·

    • historic inn · Kalamazoo

      The Kalamazoo House

      David and Amelia Lilienfeld, of a cigar-making family, built the Victorian house in 1878. It was set for demolition in 1985 for a parking lot until restorers Lou and Annette Conti saved it, and it has been an inn nearly ever since.

      An Italianate mansion inn near downtown, named after the city's first hotel.

      447 W. South St., Kalamazoo, MI 49007

      Nearby: Bronson Park Historic District, next door

      Their website › · Directions · ·

    • B&B · South Haven

      Carriage House at the Harbor

      J. F. Crowley built the house in 1886 as the home of Henry and Ellen Avery.

      A harbor-side B&B a short walk from the river and downtown.

      118 Woodman Ave., South Haven, MI 49090

      Their website › · Directions

    • B&B · South Havencheck before you go

      Yelton Manor

      Its guest house was built on the foundation of the old Dewey Hotel, beside the main manor, which was designed as a large family home.

      An adults-only boutique B&B across from the beach.

      140 N. Shore Dr., South Haven, MI 49090

      Nearby: Michigan Maritime Museum, 0.2 mi away

      Their website › · Directions · ·

    Indiana

    • historic hotel · Elkhart

      Hotel Elkhart

      The hotel was built in 1923 and restored over more than two years, keeping its plaster lobby ceilings, terrazzo floors and ballroom. It reopened in summer 2021 as part of Hilton's Tapestry Collection.

      A 93-room downtown hotel with a corner bar, a rooftop bar and a café.

      500 S. Main St., Elkhart, IN 46516

      Nearby: Lerner Theatre, next door

      Their website › · Directions · ·

    • B&B and tea house · Nappanee

      Market St. Manor

      This is the Queen Anne home of Frank and Katharine Coppes, built from 1887 to 1893. Frank and his brother John founded the Coppes cabinet company in 1876, and the house has been on the National Register since 1990.

      A pink Victorian mansion B&B that also serves afternoon tea.

      302 E. Market St., Nappanee, IN 46550

      Nearby: Coppes Commons and Coppes History Museum, next door

      Their website › · Directions · ·

    • historic inn · La Portecheck before you go

      Arbor Hill Inn

      The inn fills a large La Porte house built in 1910 and renovated in 2000; one of its suites is still known as Mrs. Jones’ room.

      A country inn with themed rooms and suites on three floors, plus a guest house, that also serves dinners to guests.

      263 W. Johnson Rd., La Porte, IN 46350

      Their website › · Directions

    • B&B · South Bend

      Oliver Inn Bed & Breakfast

      James Oliver, inventor of the Oliver Chilled Plow, had this 1886 Queen Anne mansion built as a wedding gift for his daughter. South Bend's preservation commission rates it an "outstanding" historic structure.

      A ten-room Victorian B&B a short walk from downtown on West Washington Street.

      630 W. Washington St., South Bend, IN 46601

      Nearby: Studebaker-Bendix Heritage Trail, next door

      Their website › · Directions · ·
