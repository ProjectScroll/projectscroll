    Michigan

    • diner · Hickory Corners

      George & Sally's Blue Moon Diner (Gilmore Car Museum)

      This 1941 diner was restored in 2004 and moved more than 800 miles from Meriden, Connecticut, to the Gilmore Car Museum, where it opened in 2005.

      Hot dogs, blue-plate specials, pie and frozen custard in a real streamlined diner on the museum grounds; open with the museum’s season.

      6865 W. Hickory Rd., Hickory Corners, MI 49060

      Nearby: Gilmore Car Museum, next door

      Their website › · Directions · ·

    • brewpub · Benton Harbor

      The Livery

      The brewpub is in a restored 1800s building in the Arts District that once housed the Palace Livery, a horse livery stable.

      Craft beer, pub food and live music in an old brick livery.

      190 5th St., Benton Harbor, MI 49022

      Nearby: Morton House Museum, 0.4 mi away

      Their website › · Directions · ·

    • restaurant and cidery · Buchanan

      Thistledown & Row

      It occupies the Redden Building, which brothers Samuel and Levi Redden built in 1884 and which then stood empty for years. The building was restored after 2020, and the restaurant opened on January 1, 2025.

      Modern American food with house-made ciders, meads and Michigan wines in a restored brick building downtown.

      103 Days Ave., Buchanan, MI 49107

      Nearby: Pears Mill, next door

      Their website › · Directions · ·

    • restaurant · Colomacheck before you go

      Blackbird Waterhouse

      George Klotter opened this building in the late 1920s as the Wil-O-Paw Station and Inn, a barbecue stand with a car repair shop out front, and by the 1940s it was a supper club. The Immers bought and renovated it in 2018.

      Smoked meats, steaks and live music in an old roadhouse.

      4896 Paw Paw Lake Rd., Coloma, MI 49038

      Nearby: North Berrien Historical Museum, 1.6 mi away

      Their website › · Directions · ·

    • orchard farm market · Coloma

      Jollay Orchards

      The family has farmed this land since 1857, and the eighth generation now works it.

      A u-pick fruit farm whose market sells fresh cider, baked goods and apple-cinnamon doughnuts.

      1850 Friday Rd., Coloma, MI 49038

      Nearby: North Berrien Historical Museum, 2.4 mi away

      Their website › · Directions · ·

      Oink's Dutch Treat

      A pig-themed ice cream parlor with a long list of flavors.

    • burger tavern · New Buffalo

      Redamak's

      George and Gladys Redamak started the place in the late 1940s. Jim and Angie Maroney took it over on July 15, 1975, and their family still runs it.

      A Harbor Country burger institution open about eight months a year.

      616 E. Buffalo St., New Buffalo, MI 49117

      Nearby: Harbor Country Museum of History + Rail (formerly New Buffalo Railroad Museum), 0.6 mi away

    • bakery · Stevensville

      Bit of Swiss Pastry Shoppe

      When St. Joseph's Whitcomb Hotel closed in 1966, Emil Tosi bought its bakeshop equipment and set it up in a cabin on his family's property. The hotel's former baker, Hans Kottmann, opened the bakery there in 1967.

      A European-style pastry shop known for tortes, pastries and breads.

      4333 Ridge Rd., Stevensville, MI 49127

      Their website › · Directions · ·

      Grande Mere Inn

    • tavern · Cassopolischeck before you go

      Holden Green Tavern

      The building was the village post office from 1897 to 1957 and later held a barbershop and a thrift shop. Steve and Shelley Schroeder bought it in 2018 and opened the tavern in 2021, keeping an old telephone booth and barber chairs more than 100 years old.

      A cozy downtown tavern known for its pizza and sandwiches, with old photos of Cassopolis on the walls.

      151 S. Broadway St., Cassopolis, MI 49031

      Nearby: Sinclair Station, next door

    • candy shop and soda fountain · Dowagiac

      Caruso's Candy & Soda Fountain

      Antonio and Emilia Caruso opened the shop in September 1922, and it is now run by their granddaughter, the third generation of the family. The building dates from about 1874, and a candy and ice cream business called the Chicago Candy Kitchen was already on the site by 1910.

      An old-fashioned soda fountain where candy is still cooled on a big marble table, known for homemade chocolates and ice cream.

      130 S. Front St., Dowagiac, MI 49047

      Nearby: Heddon Museum, next door

      No website: call ahead · Directions · ·

    • Italian restaurant · Dowagiac

      Wood Fire Trattoria

      The restaurant fills a downtown Dowagiac building that the tourist council describes as about 150 years old, which had been a restaurant before Wood Fire's founders took it over.

      Wood-fired pizza and Italian dinners in an old Front Street storefront next door to Caruso's.

      134 S. Front St., Dowagiac, MI 49047

      Nearby: Heddon Museum, next door

      Their website › · Directions · ·

    • tavern · Dowagiac

      Wounded Minnow Saloon

      The saloon fills the ground floor of the Elks (BPOE Lodge 889) Temple, which opened in 1913 with the lodge's lounge, meeting rooms, pool hall and theater on the two floors above. Over the years the street-level space held grocery stores, the state liquor store and taverns, including Hook's, before the Wounded Minnow.

      A casual downtown tavern serving sandwiches, burgers and drinks, named for a classic local fishing lure.

      234 S. Front St., Dowagiac, MI 49047

      Nearby: Dowagiac District Library – Genealogy & Local History, next door

      No website: call ahead · Directions · ·

    • tavern · Sumnervillecheck before you go

      Old Tavern Inn

      It opened in 1835, before Michigan became a state, as a stagecoach stop with food, drink and beds, and later held the town’s meetings and dances. It has been sided, added onto and turned to face a new way, but its original beams survive in the basement, and Michigan counts it as the state’s oldest business still running in its first building.

      A small, old-time tavern known for its burgers, with old photographs of the building on its walls.

      61088 Indian Lake Rd., Niles, MI 49120

      Their website › · Directions · ·

    • brewpub · Kalamazoo

      Bell's Eccentric Café

      Larry Bell started a homebrew supply shop in 1983 and began selling his own beer in 1985. The Eccentric Café opened next to the brewery in 1993, and Discover Kalamazoo calls it the first brewery pub in Michigan.

      A craft-beer landmark with a full kitchen, a beer garden and a music hall.

      355 E. Kalamazoo Ave., Kalamazoo, MI 49007

      Nearby: Kalamazoo Valley Museum, 0.3 mi away

      Their website › · Directions · ·

    • tavern · Kalamazoo

      Louie's Trophy House

      Louie's says it has served the community since 1918, starting as a soup kitchen, and calls the Trophy House Kalamazoo's oldest bar.

      A bar and grill with walls full of mounted game, which gives it the feel of a northwoods cabin.

      629 Walbridge St., Kalamazoo, MI 49007

      Nearby: Kalamazoo Valley Museum, 0.5 mi away

      Their website › · Directions · ·

    • restaurant and saloon · South Haven

      Clementine's

      The restaurant fills the former Citizens Bank building, built in about 1897 and refurbished with its period look intact. One family has run it since 1982.

      A busy downtown spot known for burgers and onion rings under the old bank's high ceilings.

      500 Phoenix St., South Haven, MI 49090

      Nearby: Michigan Maritime Museum, 0.2 mi away

    • ice cream parlor · South Havencheck before you go

      Sherman's of South Haven

      Sherman's started as a dairy in 1916, turned to ice cream in the 1950s and opened its South Haven stand in 1958. After 66 seasons it closed in fall 2024, then reopened in 2025 under new owner Mike Schimanski.

      A classic summer ice cream stand famous for towering scoops.

      1601 Phoenix St., South Haven, MI 49090

    • orchard farm market · Goshencheck before you go

      Kercher's Sunrise Orchards

      The Kercher family came from Ohio in 1851, and William Wheeler Kercher started growing apples commercially on 40 acres in 1922. The fourth generation, Tom and Maureen Kercher, runs the orchard with their children.

      A fall farm stop with apples, pumpkins, a corn maze, wagon rides and a market of homemade goods.

      19498 County Road 38, Goshen, IN 46526

      Their website › · Directions

    • diner and soda fountain · Goshen

      South Side Soda Shop & Diner

      The building opened in 1910 as a grocery that also held a post office. It became a soda shop in 1940, closed in the early 1980s, and reopened as the South Side Soda Shop in 1986; the diner-car addition came around 1994.

      A retro family diner known for its chili, shakes and malts, and in-house pies, and seen on TV's Diners, Drive-Ins and Dives.

      1122 S. Main St., Goshen, IN 46526

      Nearby: Mennonite Historical Library (Goshen College), 0.7 mi away

      Their website › · Directions · ·

      Indiana's big family-style Amish restaurant, famous for noodles, fried chicken and pies.

    • food hall in a historic factory · Nappanee

      Coppes Commons

      The complex fills the 1887 factory where the Coppes company, founded in 1876 by Frank and John Coppes, built the famous "Hoosier" kitchen cabinets and shipped them across the country.

      A restored cabinet factory with a coffee shop, deli, bakery, pretzels, ice cream and a small cabinet museum.

      401 E. Market St., Nappanee, IN 46550

      Nearby: Coppes Commons and Coppes History Museum, next door

      Their website › · Directions · ·

    • Amish-style restaurant and bakery · Shipshewana

      Blue Gate Restaurant

      Mel and June Riegsecker bought and fixed up an old Shipshewana factory building in 1984. In the mid-1980s they opened a 50-seat restaurant called Der Strudel Haus, which grew into the Blue Gate.

      Family-style Amish cooking and pies in the center of Shipshewana, with a theater next door.

    • tavern · Mishawakacheck before you go

      Midway Tavern (Martha's Midway)

      The tavern turned 100 in October 2024. A Belgian immigrant family took it over in the early years, Martha ran it until she died at 91, and her descendants run it now. The barroom was remodeled in 1933 and looks much as it did then.

      A neighborhood bar with a back room known for live blues.

      West Fourth St. near Smith St., Mishawaka, IN

      Barnaby's

      George and Ruby Stratigos opened Barnaby's in 1969, and their son John runs it today. It stands where his grandparents built a brownstone house in 1920; his grandfather Spyridon also ran the Stratigon Grille downtown from 1914 to 1963.

      A neighborhood pizza and sandwich spot across from Howard Park in the East Bank.

      713 E. Jefferson Blvd., South Bend, IN 46617

    • Italian restaurant · South Bendcheck before you go

      Sunny Italy Cafe

      It opened in 1926 as the North Niles Avenue Cafe, and Notre Dame students nicknamed it "Rosie's" after owner Rosie Vumbaca. Jimmy and Josie Bamber bought it in 1940 and named it Sunny Italy.

      South Bend's oldest Italian restaurant, a red-checkered-tablecloth place with old-school charm.
