    The whole valley, on one map

    The valley map

    Every place on Project Scroll that has a pin: 98 museums and historic places, 27 stories, 55 festivals, 39 places to eat and stay, 33 outdoor places, the walk stops, the old postcards, the lost towns and the old town plans. Pick a day to see what is open, switch the layers on and off, and open any pin for its story.

    My day

    The places

      “Open now” and the day you pick use each museum’s posted hours, and each festival’s 2026 dates; places with no posted hours drop out when a day is chosen. “Free” means free admission as posted. Your day list and passport stay on this device. Map pins come from each street address. The base map, with its town names, highways and street names, and the aerial photographs are from the USGS National Map, a free public-domain service of the US Geological Survey; your browser fetches its map pieces from the USGS while the map is open (choose “Plain” to stop that). County lines: US Census Bureau; rivers: USGS. “Near me” asks your browser for your location and uses it only on this page. “Directions” opens Google Maps. Hours and dates change: check each place before you go.
