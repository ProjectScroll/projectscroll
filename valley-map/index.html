    The whole valley, on one map

    The valley map

    Every place on Project Scroll that has a pin: 98 museums and historic places, 39 places to eat and stay, 55 festivals, the walk stops, the lost towns and the old town plans. Switch the layers on and off, find what is near you, and open any pin for its story.

    The places

      Map pins come from each street address. County lines: US Census Bureau; rivers: USGS. Only if you press “Show today’s streets” does your browser ask OpenStreetMap’s tile server for street maps, under its privacy policy. “Near me” asks your browser for your location and uses it only on this page. Hours and dates change: check each place before you go.
