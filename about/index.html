    About Project Scroll

    Project Scroll is independent historical research: a history hunt anyone can play, and a research desk anyone can hire. Both run on one rule. Every clue is true, and every answer is in the record.

    Who we are

    Project Scroll is run by an independent researcher and author who writes narrative nonfiction about the history of the Great Lakes country, and who was trained in visual communications. The research behind those books is the same research we offer you: old certificates, deeds, surveys, county histories and newspapers, read line by line and compared until they agree, or until we can say exactly why they don’t.

    What we believe

    • The record decides. Not the family tree online, not the story everybody tells, not what we hoped to find.
    • Every statement is marked. You can always see whether a fact comes from a document, a book, a family story or our own reading. How we mark the evidence.
    • Gaps are shown, not filled. An honest “not yet known” is worth more than a confident guess.
    • Corrections are welcome. If you know better, tell us. The record is bigger than any one of us.
    • Places and people are respected. The hunt never asks anyone to dig, to disturb a grave or a mound, or to go looking for anyone’s home.

    Where it started

    Project Scroll began in Cassopolis, Michigan, with a few questions the county history couldn’t answer. It doesn’t stop there. The hunt and the desk now take on questions from anywhere the records reach.
