    How to play

    1. Find a clue. Every mystery is posted on Project Scroll’s Facebook page, and each case has its own page with the first sources to open.
    2. Search the record. Old books, maps and certificates, many of them free online.
    3. Post your answer. In the comments, saying exactly where you found it: the book, the page, and a link.
    4. Get named. The first solver is named in the next post and on the case board, for good.

    No case has been cracked yet. The first name on the board could be yours.

    The rules

    • Read the record. Every answer is in a book, on a map, or on a page.
    • Show your work. A name without the page it came from is only a guess.
    • Stay on paper. Never dig, never go near a grave, and never set foot on anyone’s land.
    • Be kind. These are real people, and their families are still here.
