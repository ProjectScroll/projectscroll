          Before you go

          • Check the hours. Many of these places are run by volunteers and open only some days or some seasons. Their own websites have the latest.
          • Researchers, write ahead. Archives and research rooms often keep their records off the floor. A note a week before lets them have your boxes ready.
          • Bring what you know. Names, dates, the township, and a copy of anything you found here. Staff can do far more with a good question.
          • Make a day of it. Load a day trip or add a few places to My trip, press “Best order”, then use “Directions for the whole trip”. Share it with a link or a QR code. Mark the ones you have seen with “Been there”: three earns a badge for your field book, and pressing “I’m here” at the door stamps your passport.
          • No signal out there? Press “Save for no-signal use” before you leave, and this page, its map and your trip will open on your phone without a connection.
          • Know of a place we missed? Tell us and we will add it.
